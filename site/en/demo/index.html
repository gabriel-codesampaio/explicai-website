1 Add a bill

Use this screen to record an expense before it is due.

  1. Open Accounts payable in the side menu.
  2. Click New bill.
  3. Fill in supplier, amount and due date. The bill shows in the list as Unpaid.

Open the image at full size1Open Accounts payable2Click New bill3The bill shows as Unpaid

Result: the bill stays in the list and goes into the month's report.
